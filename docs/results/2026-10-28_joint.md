# Hybrid round 2: joint hypotheses (2026-10-28, dev, last round before the freeze)

Dev results: the hybrid was changed after its clean-holdout results had been seen. The final unseen test will
be a second circuit. All scores at pole level (see [2026-10-28_pole_level.md](2026-10-28_pole_level.md)).

## What changed

1. **Joint hypotheses (fault x wiring)** in the information-gain planner (`src/probeforge/baseline/infogain.py`,
   variants from `src/probeforge/hybrid/uncertainty.py`). A candidate is (fault, unknown parameters, wiring
   variant); variants are alternative wirings of the regions the transcription flagged (the terminals on one side
   of same-kind parts of a component, in any order). They are added only if they reproduce every reading so far:
   - on a conflict, before any correction is requested: every flagged region, one component's pins at a time, with
     the faults alive before the conflict plus that component's faults;
   - as soon as the surviving faults are one component in one mode with parts in a flagged region (before any
     decision to switch the power off, which cannot be undone): that region's variants with the same component's
     faults of that mode.
   With variants present the planner counts distinct faults, not candidates, when it scores a reading, adds readings
   across each part's own two terminals (at rest or while a button is held), and a two-step option (stop, then a
   start button held while reading across a part) for contacts that only close during start-up. A specific pole is
   reported only when one fault remains; otherwise the pole is dropped with "confirm wiring at ..." and the UI asks a
   human.
2. **Corrections only inside flagged regions** (`src/probeforge/hybrid/repair.py`): the prompt lists the flagged
   regions; a proposal that rewires a terminal outside them without naming a new region (with a reason) is rejected;
   proposals that pass are still checked by the acceptance rule. After two rejected proposals in a case no more are
   requested.
3. **"Without Muse Spark" rescored at pole level**: the PNG run-2 no-repair run is 10/38 at pole level as at
   component level (it hands over instead of guessing); the new no-repair runs below use the same scoring.

The info-gain baseline (no variants) gives identical results on all 43 cases after the change
(runs/20261028_220727_infogain_clean). An earlier version added the variants only once a single fault was left;
by then the planner had often switched the power off for resistance checks, so the pole could not be checked live
(free no-repair runs: 25/38 and 23/38 with 8 and 6 poles left open). Adding them before the power-off decision
gave the results below.
